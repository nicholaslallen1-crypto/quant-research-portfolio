# Public scope

This repository is an interview-safe snapshot.

It is intended to show research design, validation discipline and the ability to turn a research process into clear, reproducible interfaces. It is not a mirror of the private repositories.

## Included

- public-facing architecture notes;
- methodology summaries;
- a standalone synthetic validation example;
- conservative statements about implementation evidence.

## Excluded

- production source and proprietary algorithms;
- raw or derived market data;
- private workbooks, cached research artefacts and result exports;
- credentials, API keys, service-account material and private URLs;
- local filesystem paths and deployment-specific configuration.

The synthetic example is new, self-contained and uses generated data. It should not be interpreted as a representation of private strategy logic or as evidence of future profitability.
