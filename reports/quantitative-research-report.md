# Quantitative research report

## Scope

This is a public-safe summary of quantitative research and engineering work completed inside Allen & Cooper. It is designed to show the research questions and validation discipline without publishing production source, raw market data, exact thresholds, trade-level outputs or private performance figures.

## Macro edge discovery

The private workflow explores macro signals across a multi-asset universe while keeping discovery separate from proof. Candidate families are consolidated, observations are aligned to explicit dates, overlap and sample depth are measured, and surviving relationships are challenged with temporal splits, matched random-date baselines and benchmark comparisons.

The system also records lineage, cache fingerprints, promotion decisions and failure states. This makes it possible to distinguish a genuinely repeated research result from an adjacent-threshold duplicate, stale cache reuse or a relationship that only appeared because of event overlap.

## Trade research engine

The related research engine enforces a fixed experiment boundary:

1. Data readiness checks coverage, duplicates, session consistency, OHLC integrity and provenance.
2. TRAIN discovers a bounded set of structures.
3. VALIDATION ranks a constrained shortlist using costs, dependence and robustness diagnostics.
4. A pre-test manifest freezes the selected structure and exposure.
5. FINAL TEST can confirm, review or veto the frozen structure, but cannot select a replacement.

Diagnostics include parameter-neighbourhood stability, walk-forward analysis, temporal and regime checks, stress scenarios, effective sample size, empirical tails and Monte Carlo paths. Ruin paths and unavailable metrics remain visible rather than being discarded or replaced with invented precision.

## Research standard

Promising research results are treated as hypotheses until they survive independent validation. A public CV may reference selected results with the appropriate caveat, but this repository intentionally does not publish the underlying figures or enough detail to reconstruct a private signal.

## Limitations

Daily OHLC cannot reveal intrabar order. Macro relationships can be non-stationary. Random baselines and Monte Carlo scenarios are diagnostic tools rather than guarantees. The public report documents the method, not a live track record or investment recommendation.
